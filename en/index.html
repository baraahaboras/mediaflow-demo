Your organization's newsroom

One place for your activities, photos and consents. Every story is checked before it is used, and every report is built from approved material.

Log in

01 — Activities and photos

Every activity, with its photos

Record an activity once, add its photos, and find them again by project, activity or date.

  • Projects, and the activities under each project
  • A photo library with search

02 — Consent

Consent stays with the photo

Every person in a photo has a record of what they agreed to. A photo cannot be approved while its records do not allow it.

  • A record for each person, with what they agreed to
  • A daily check that flags consents that have expired

03 — Review

Nothing is used until someone else has checked it

Content moves from draft to review to approved, and the person who wrote it cannot approve it.

  • A reviewer approves, or asks for changes
  • Every step is written to an audit log

04 — Reports

Reports from approved material only

Build a donor report from approved content, preview it, then export it to Excel or print it.

  • A preview before anything is exported
  • The names of people in photos never appear in a report
